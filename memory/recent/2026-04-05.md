## Sponge tray — sink-mounted holder with 31mm bore

Arthur asked to reproduce and modify a sink-mounted sponge holder STL. The design has a cylindrical sleeve that slides over the sink fixture (faucet base/divider) with a sponge tray hanging off one side.

**Source STLs:**
- `sponge_tray_narrow_31mm.stl` — narrower sponge tray, 31mm internal cylinder diameter (fits Arthur's sink fixture)
- `vertical_sponge_tray_for_ikea_svampig.stl` — IKEA Svampig variant — wider sponge tray with honeycomb drainage walls, but 30mm cylinder (too narrow)

**Goal:** Take the svampig version (wider tray) and widen the bore from 30mm to 31mm to fit Arthur's fixture.

**Approach:**
1. Initial attempt created a parametric rectangular tray in OpenSCAD (`sponge-tray.scad`) — this was WRONG. The design isn't a simple rectangular tray; it's a cylinder-plus-tray shape.
2. Correct approach: imported the svampig STL into OpenSCAD, identified the cylinder axis (Y-axis, centered at X=0, Z=0), and cut a 31mm bore using a difference operation.
3. Verified alignment with a red 30mm reference cylinder overlay before cutting.

**Output:** `~/Desktop/sponge_tray_svampig_31mm.stl` — svampig's wider tray with honeycomb drainage, bore widened from 30mm to 31mm.

**Note:** The parametric `sponge-tray.scad` in the repo is the initial wrong recreation (simple rectangular tray, not the actual cylinder+tray design). The actual deliverable was an STL-level modification.
