# 2026-04-02

## W Magazine Stand — Design & Bake-off

Designed a Gridfinity W-shaped magazine stand inspired by the R&B Shelter magazine rack, scaled down to fit the Bambu A1 build volume.

### Design Decisions
- **Approach A chosen:** Flat-bottomed W (one piece) — rectangular Gridfinity base slab with W-shape growing upward
- **Grid:** 6×4 (252×168mm), print on its side (252mm width goes vertical)
- **Center spine:** 217mm tall above 10mm platform (7mm base_h + 3mm floor_h), 4mm thick
- **Outer walls:** Shorter than center spine, angled outward at 12° lean
- **Pocket floors:** Curved valleys transitioning from base to angled walls
- **Top edges:** Bullnose (rounded), no lip treatment (doesn't translate to W-shape)
- **Wall thickness:** 3-4mm throughout
