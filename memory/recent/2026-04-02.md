### Design decisions
- Tangent-blend valley replacement over Bezier or global `offset`/`minkowski` (more predictable, targeted)
- Broader rounding via outer-wall profile reshaping, not global smoothing (preserves center spine crispness)
- Two-pass approach: inner valleys first, broader rounding second, each with user approval before proceeding
